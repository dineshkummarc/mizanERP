# Return Pack

The year's personal return, assembled from your own books: every figure IRIS asks for, under the heading
it asks for it by, plus the wealth statement and its reconciliation.

## What it reads

The personal ledger for the chosen tax year. A tax year runs July to June and FBR names it for the year it
ends in, so this app's 2025-2026 is FBR's Tax Year 2026.

## What the pack shows <!-- requires: PersonalFinanceView -->

Modelled line by line on a real 114(1) print:

- **Income by head** — every head with the four-column split IRIS uses: total, subject to final tax,
  subject to exemption, subject to normal tax. Each row carries its IRIS code.
- **Computations** — the block that runs from 9000 to 9203 in the order the portal shows them.
  Final-regime income, such as export of services under s.154A or the flat capital-gains charge, never
  joins taxable income; its tax sits in the fixed and final block beside the slab tax on what remains.
- **Withholding by section** — what was deducted at source, by the section it was deducted under, to
  claim against the tax due.
- **How the slab tax was worked out** — the schedule applied, slab by slab.
- **Wealth statement** and **reconciliation of net assets** — opening wealth, the year's income and
  outflows, and closing wealth, in the shape IRIS reconciles them.

Exemption is a column of zeros until an exempt regime exists; it is printed anyway, so the pack's shape
is the return's shape.

## The PDF

**Download PDF** prints the pack for the person who files. FBR has no filing API, so nothing here
transmits: the last step is you on iris.fbr.gov.pk with these codes beside you, or a practitioner handed
the PDF. A personal account's own screen — business books must never be read as one person's taxable
income, which is why a company does not see it.
